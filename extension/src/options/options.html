Settings

Protection

Enable protection Master switch. Turn this off and AvoPseudo does nothing at all.
Pseudonymise when I paste Text pasted into a chat box (Ctrl/⌘+V, right-click Paste, paste buttons) is checked and pseudonymised before it lands in the box. A panel then tells you exactly what was replaced.
Also check when I send (legacy) Extra safety net that rewrites the prompt as it is sent, catching sensitive data you typed rather than pasted. Off by default: the change happens invisibly, after you have hit send.
On-device name detection A small language model, running entirely in your browser, that spots people and organisations no pattern can describe. It makes each paste slower, and downloads the model once on first use.

What happens to detected data — anonymize, warn or block — is decided by the detection rules below, not a global mode.

Detection rules

Each rule decides what to look for and what to do — anonymize, warn or block. Turn rules on or off below. To change what a rule detects, click Copy, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, then paste the assistant's reply back into the box at the bottom. Every change is saved automatically.

Always protect

Client, matter or project names that must always be protected — the things no generic rule can guess. Add one whenever you open a sensitive file.

Never flag

Values that should never be detected (safe domains, test addresses, internal IPs…).

Rules

Copy (or New rule with AI) puts a ready-made prompt on your clipboard. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, replace the <<…>> part with what you want, send it, then paste the assistant's reply into the box below.

    Apply a change from ChatGPT / Claude

    Paste the rule (or a full rules file) the assistant gave you and click Apply. A single rule is merged in by its name; a full file replaces everything.

    Advanced — edit the raw YAML

    For technical users. Editing here replaces the entire ruleset.
